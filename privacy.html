Privacy

This site has no accounts, no sign-ups and no forms, and it never sells or shares anyone's information. Here is what it does with data, all of it.

Last updated November 7, 2026

Visitor counts

The site counts page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't follow anyone to other sites and only reports totals, like how many people opened the football page this week.

What stays on your device

If you choose Girls or Boys on the soccer site, your browser remembers that choice for your next visit. The site also keeps copies of pages you've already opened, so scores still load with a weak signal in the stands. A couple of short notes, like whether you just came from the home page, disappear when you close the tab. All of it stays in your browser, and clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes it.

Game-day weather

Forecasts on game days come from Open-Meteo. The site asks for the forecast in the town where the game is played. It never asks for your location.

Newsletters

When you open an Athletic Department newsletter here, the site tells Smore, the service the department publishes it with, that the issue was viewed, so the department's view count includes readers on this site. Smore's own privacy policy covers that request.

Fonts

The typefaces load from Google Fonts. Like any website request, that shares basic technical details such as your IP address with Google.

Links to other sites

Links to Hudl, MaxPreps, YouTube, Instagram, the MIAA, news sites and map apps open those services, and their privacy policies apply once you're there. Calendar subscriptions download a schedule file from this site and send nothing back.

Questions

Students in the Danvers High School DECA chapter built and run this site. For anything about the teams themselves, contact the Athletic Department through Falcons Athletics.
