    Line up against one of the fastest Falcons on file in the 55 meters. Wait for the gun, then tap as fast as you can. Go early and it's a false start.

    0.0 m/s

    Tap the button, or press Space or the arrow keys; alternating two fingers is fastest. The Falcon runs their real time with a standard sprint start, and your clock starts at the gun, so your reaction time counts, just like a real race.

    Dual Meet Scoring

    How a track meet is won: step through a real Danvers dual meet event by event and watch the score add up to the official result.

    How a dual meet is scored

    In each individual event the top three from the two teams score 5, 3 and 1 points. In a relay the faster team's relay scores 5. At an NEC meet several dual meets run at once, so a Falcon's place among the two teams can differ from their place overall.

        About the data

        Races, lap splits and dual meets come from the official results Lancer Timing posted for the Northeastern Conference meets of Dec. 11, 2022, Jan. 22, 2023 and Dec. 9, 2023, and from the MIAA's results for the 2024 and 2026 Division 4 meets and Meets of Champions. The other features use every Danvers result on this site. The two dual meets add up to the team scores Lancer Timing posted, and the state meet points add up to each official team score.
